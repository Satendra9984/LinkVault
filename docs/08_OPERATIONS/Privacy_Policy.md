## 2. Local storage and cloud sync

- **Guest / local mode:** Data may be stored only on your device.
- **Signed-in / cloud mode:** Data may be stored and synchronized with our backend so you can use the service across devices, subject to your plan and quotas.

## 3. Third-party services

LinkVault may integrate services such as:

- Authentication and database (e.g. Supabase)
- Subscriptions (e.g. RevenueCat)
- Advertising (e.g. Google AdMob)
- Other infrastructure as configured in the app

Each provider processes data under its own terms. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

- You may request deletion of your account and associated cloud data where the app and backend support it (e.g. account deletion flow).
- Local data can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app, subject to your device OS behavior.

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard practices such as encryption in transit for network communication where applicable. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Children’s privacy

LinkVault is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
